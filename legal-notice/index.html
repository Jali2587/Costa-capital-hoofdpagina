Services Provided

Costa Capital provides commercial real estate finance brokerage and intermediary services to professional and corporate borrowers. Our services include:

  • Assessment and structuring of financing requests
  • Presentation of financing opportunities to professional lenders
  • Process management from initial assessment through closing
  • Bridge finance, acquisition finance, development finance and refinancing intermediation

Important Disclaimer

Costa Capital does not:

  • Provide loans itself
  • Accept deposits or investment funds from the public
  • Make credit decisions or loan approvals
  • Arrange consumer credit or owner-occupied residential mortgages
  • Offer investment products or guarantee investment returns

Independent Lenders: All financing is provided by independent third-party lenders. Each lender conducts its own underwriting, due diligence, valuation, KYC and credit approval, and determines its own financing terms independently of Costa Capital.
